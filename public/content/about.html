About PCEE

The Parallel Coordinates Explorable Explainer (PCEE) is an interactive tutorial about parallel coordinates. Parallel coordinates are a powerful visualisation technique used to explore and analyse multidimensional tabular data.

This web site is based on the PCEE Main Project and is intended for research on website visibility, search engine optimization (SEO), answer engine optimization (AEO) and generative engine optimization (GEO).
